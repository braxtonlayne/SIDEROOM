## Owner decisions

For each of these, the recommended answer was adopted as the default and is being implemented. The owner confirms it or overrides it.

1. **UnRAR licence for RAR/CBR reading.** (D-170) The `unrar` crate vendors RARLAB's UnRAR source (crate `unrar_sys`), whose manifest says `MIT OR Apache-2.0` but whose actual licence (`vendor/unrar/license.txt` in the crate) is the RARLAB freeware licence: free to use "in any software to handle RAR archives without limitations", not usable to build a RAR-compatible archiver, and modified UnRAR source distributed with other software must carry its licence paragraph. SIDEROOM only decompresses, so it fits a self-hosted deployment and RAR reading is implemented and on by default. The default adopted: keep it on behind `library.ingest.rar_enabled`, and include the UnRAR licence text in release notices. `cargo deny` cannot see this (it trusts the manifest). The owner confirms, or switches RAR off for good by deleting the `unrar` dependency (7z and ZIP are unaffected). — [crates/sr-library/Cargo.toml](../crates/sr-library/Cargo.toml)

11. **P15-RST-2 JASSUB licence for ASS subtitles** (legal, and a download). `jassub` is LGPL-2.1-or-later JavaScript around a WASM build of libass (ISC), FreeType (FTL), HarfBuzz (MIT), fribidi (LGPL-2.1), fontconfig and expat (MIT); nothing GPL in the shipped form. Recommended default: add it pinned and unmodified as its own lazily loaded chunk with the worker and `.wasm` served as separate files (which satisfies the LGPL's relinking condition in a browser), its licence texts in the release notices and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES`, and a `pnpm licenses` allow-list check in `web.yml` naming it, the same treatment `pdfjs-dist` (D-217) and `foliate-js` (D-225) got. Until the owner answers nothing is added: the seam in `apps/web/src/cinema/player/ass.ts` stays and ASS tracks play through the WebVTT conversion. The owner approves the licence and the download, or closes CIN-PLY-004 as WebVTT-only. — [apps/web/src/cinema/player/ass.ts](../apps/web/src/cinema/player/ass.ts) **2026-10-16 update: the owner approved the licence and the download, but the audit for K-13 found that `jassub` 2.5.16 has a hard dependency, `rvfc-polyfill` 1.0.8 (a requestVideoFrameCallback polyfill that `jassub.js` imports unconditionally), whose LICENSE and package.json are GPL-3.0. That contradicts "nothing GPL in the shipped form", so nothing was added; the owner must re-confirm (see docs/dev/handoff/feat-p15-jassub.md).**
