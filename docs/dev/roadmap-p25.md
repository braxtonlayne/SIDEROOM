The four unticked ones are all owner items: RDY-VOX-002 (the ten-member soak on a real machine; eight members for ten minutes passed on the standard runner), RDY-PAY-001 (the signet wallet), RDY-DOC-003 (walking the playbooks on a running stack) and RDY-DEV-003 (two downloads, after the first scheduled `sdk-compat` run on 2026-10-26). The merged wave-24 tree passes 3,500 Rust tests, 2,630 web unit tests, the three-browser Playwright suite and, on CI, the media, storage, voice-relay, load-test and prod-smoke jobs. Across inventories 01 to 19 nothing is unticked except two won't-builds (D-257, D-261) and DEV-SDK-003.

The rules of [roadmap-p20.md](roadmap-p20.md) section 2 apply unchanged: worktrees `C:\Users\braxt\Desktop\SIDEROOM_wt\p<wave>-<agent>` on `feat/p<wave>-<agent>` with their own `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`, scratch database `sr_p<wave>_<agent>` and ports; one cargo build at a time per agent (`-j 2`); ten migration numbers per agent, unused numbers stay unused; the append-only shared files; generated files regenerated by the integrator; one i18n fragment per agent (`p<wave>-<agent>-<n>.ts`); a handoff `docs/dev/handoff/feat-p<wave>-<agent>.md` with "Resume here"; integration on `integration/phase<wave>` with the full gate list; nothing pushed, merged or downloaded without the owner; proof over claims.
