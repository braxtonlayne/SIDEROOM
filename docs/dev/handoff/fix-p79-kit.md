## Item 2 (310)
- Confirm: vitest `Tabs.test.tsx` (new file beside the component; sizes faked on `HTMLElement.prototype`) fails on the base `Tabs.tsx` (1 of 2 failed: no attribute) and passes now. The shots spec on the base (Tabs.tsx and the CSS held back, stack on `p72-base-bin`), Lounge Dark, 375x812: last tab right edge 401 beyond the list's client box right 346, `data-overflow` absent, no mask (`base-saved-phone.png`, `base-measurements.txt`).
- Build: the list has a ref; `measure()` sets `data-overflow` = `start`/`end`/`both`/absent from `|scrollLeft|`, `scrollWidth`, `clientWidth` (1 px tolerance); called on mount, on `onScroll`, from a guarded `ResizeObserver` and when `items` changes. Selected tab scrolled into view on mount and when the selection changes. **Deviation from the contract's wording:** instead of `scrollIntoView({block:'nearest'})` (which can scroll the page vertically on mount when the strip is below the fold), the effect compares the tab's and the list's `getBoundingClientRect` and adjusts the list's own `scrollLeft`. Same result, never moves a parent, needs no jsdom guard.
- CSS: `scrollbar-width: none` (and `::-webkit-scrollbar` hidden); the mask is on the **list itself** (`mask-image`, 24 px, `to right` gradients, swapped to `to left` under `:dir(rtl)`). In the shots the fade does not break the underline variant visibly (the pill variant is what Saved items uses; the underline variant of Cinema's My list fits and has no mask). **RTL not shot.**
- **Found and fixed (kit, one line):** `.pill .tab:hover` outranked `.pill .tab[aria-selected='true']`, so a selected pill under the pointer turned grey with white ink (unreadable in Lounge Light right after a click). It is now `:hover:not(:disabled):not([aria-selected='true'])`. Pre-existing, not part of the contract; revert if unwanted.
- **At 1280 the Saved items popover is itself only 318 px wide**, so its strip really overflows there too: `data-overflow` is `end`/`start` at 1280 as on the phone, which the contract expected to be absent "unless a strip really overflows there". Strips that fit (Cinema My list, Friends page at both sizes) have no attribute and no mask. The spec asserts the cue matches `scrollWidth > clientWidth`.
